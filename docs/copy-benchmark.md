# Copy benchmark — inspected 14 October 2026

These are observations of live public pages returned by the browsing tool, not evidence that a phrase increases conversion. Logged-in experiences, actual checkout flows and commercial analytics of these companies were not inspected. No sentences, statistics, endorsements or trust badges were copied.

| Reference | Observed communication pattern | SiteFit application / rejection |
|---|---|---|
| [Stripe UK](https://stripe.com/gb) | Revenue-oriented proposition, product families tied to tasks; customer cases and technical depth later | Connect evidence to a lease decision. Reject enterprise breadth and unearned scale/reliability figures |
| [Linear](https://linear.app) | Names its audience and product category, then shows concrete product workflow | State property/business purpose immediately; show the report format rather than generic AI decoration |
| [Wise UK](https://wise.com/gb) | Task-labelled actions, immediately visible amounts/fees in the transfer example; relevant risk qualifications beside the affected feature | Explain free versus one-off paid report and uncertainty where it changes the decision. Do not borrow its delivery speeds or social proof |
| [Shopify UK](https://www.shopify.com/uk) | Aspirational hero plus a prominent free start, concrete selling tasks and examples | Adopt clear first action and practical benefits. Reject empire/AI superlatives inappropriate to a single-property assessment |
| [Placer.ai](https://www.placer.ai) | Property/chain/region search and tangible location use cases | Keep property-first entry. Its foot-traffic proposition is not SiteFit's capability; no transferred measurement claim |
| [MyTraffic](https://www.mytraffic.io/en) | Geographic questions and named use-case stories explain analytical applications | Frame real lease questions; reject unrestricted AI-assistant positioning and unsupported visitor counts |
| [Green Street Retail Data, formerly Local Data Company](https://eu.greenstreet.com/products/data-analytics/retail-data/) | Explicit occupier/demographic data categories and retailer/adviser use cases; brochure/demo path | Explain available business records and demographics precisely. Reject physically verified-store, viability and broad-market claims SiteFit cannot establish |

## Homepage alternatives assessed internally

1. **Risk reduction:** “Found a property? Check it before you commit.” Selected: identifies the customer's situation and moment of use, without promising to eliminate risk.
2. **Concept fit:** “Could this location work for your business?” Clear question, but can imply that SiteFit can select a winning operating format despite missing premises evidence.
3. **Commercial evidence:** “Get the evidence before you sign a lease.” Concrete timing, but could suggest completeness. Use the evidence/action explanation beneath the selected headline instead.

This is an editorial judgement about clarity and product accuracy, not a measured winner. Do not run experiments until authorised commercial operation and measurement are available.

## Prompt reference

[OpenAI structured outputs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs) distinguishes schema adherence from factual correctness and recommends explicit handling of incompatible input. The existing strict schema, unknown states and evidence slots remain unchanged; only prose style instructions are added. No provider call, prompt optimisation service or model migration was used.
