# Phase15 recovery and data-rights runbook

No Production operation or destructive customer-data change authorised. Verify identity and scope using existing verified account/owned graph; do not ask for tokens/card details. Contact info@sitefit.co.uk is owner-supplied, inbox/transactional sending is not automatically verified.

1. Restore drill: test-only isolated database, real migrations/RLS, synthetic Ready and contract data; compare report/content digests, roles/grants and immutable triggers before/after. Never restore over hosted development/customer data.
2. Real backup: after owner approves location, encryption/access and required private connection credentials, capture database and migration history separately from external Auth configuration, Vault secrets and Storage objects. Existing Free hosting is not a guarantee of downloadable managed recovery. Never place backup/credentials in Git or support tickets. Do not export actual customer data before storage approval.
3. Data request: verify applicant, locate linked identity/analyses/contracts/payment/support records; assemble a scoped access export privately. Document applicable lawful retention/holds/rights before proposing erasure or anonymisation. No automatic account delete is advertised while RESTRICT FKs and policy are unresolved.
4. Deletion execution: requires approved category policy and tested scoped operator capability. Never globally disable triggers, truncate shared tables, erase another owner or refresh historical reports. Actual destructive action requires its appropriate explicit approval. Backups require a documented expiry/reapplication process, not an untrue instant-deletion promise.
5. Cancellation: record a bounded request against the original paid purchase. Repeated reason yields one record; no new charge/refund occurs. Resolve using approved contractual/statutory policy and trusted Stripe verification; request status is not refund status. Do not automatically waive statutory rights or classify every buyer as business.
6. Confirmation: immutable full policy/consent receipt preparation is separate from provision in an approved durable medium. Only a trusted approved channel with its actual delivery receipt may mark confirmation. No sender/channel is enabled in this phase by default.
