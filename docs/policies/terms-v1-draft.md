## Failure, defects and refunds
Payment confirmation, Ready and delivery are separate states. A customer should not purchase again to resolve failed delivery. Record requests against the original payment; investigate without another automatic AI charge. Refunds are not declared issued before trusted confirmation. Failed/non-conforming supply is handled under applicable statutory remedies; contractual cancellation and defect remedies are distinct. Withdrawal requests remain receivable outside the voluntary window where statutory grounds apply. No broad no-refund or AI disclaimer excludes mandatory rights.
