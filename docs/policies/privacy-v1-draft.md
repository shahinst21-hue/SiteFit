# SiteFit Privacy Policy — privacy-v1-draft

DRAFT — not approved for launch. Owner/controller identity, correspondence address, privacy contact, applicable bases/transfer safeguards and retention schedule need approval. No fictitious company details are supplied.

## Data and purposes
SiteFit stores the selected commercial address/unit, business concept, analysis/input versions, permitted provider evidence, generated interpretation and report provenance to prepare and reopen the purchased report. Free Snapshot persistence is implemented; the old statement that all inputs remain only in browser memory is obsolete. Accounts use Supabase Auth for email/Google identity and necessary session cookies. Minimal profiles, ownership and access records are stored. Financial Engine data is separate from the Full Report.

Purchases use Stripe; SiteFit retains internal payment/session references, status, amount/currency, entitlements and limited audit events, not full card details. New purchases record versioned terms, supply request, applicable acknowledgement and buyer-purpose declaration. A business declaration alone does not remove consumer rights. Cancellation/delivery requests retain an allowlisted reason and original payment binding.

Proposed lawful-basis mapping: requested analysis/contract performance; legal obligations where applicable to financial/rights records; documented legitimate interests for proportionate security and fraud prevention. Specific mapping must be approved, not treated as blanket consent. Marketing consent is separate; no marketing analytics is introduced by Phase15.

## Recipients and transfers
Existing infrastructure/providers include Vercel hosting, Supabase database/Auth, Stripe payments, OpenAI interpretation and the approved evidence providers. Only required data is sent; AI receives bounded evidence, not account tokens/card details. Review processor terms, locations, subprocessors and applicable international-transfer safeguards before launch; do not promise UK-only processing. This draft does not certify each provider's independent factual accuracy.

## Rights and contact
Provide the approved privacy contact before launch. Verify request identity proportionately without asking for credentials. Handle applicable access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability rights and complaints. Immutable reports do not displace statutory rights. A lawful erasure/redaction workflow must distinguish necessary retained records, legal holds, licensed public evidence and identifiable customer information; no refresh/re-generation replaces historical records. Requests and decisions are audited with minimal metadata.

Retention follows the approved category schedule; undefined periods are not an unlimited-storage promise. Necessary cookies/security logging are described separately from future optional marketing. Update this notice when actual processing changes, preserving the terms/privacy version associated with each contract.
