# Guest purchase sign-in and continuation — 28 October 2026

The owner reported both Google and email failures after a signed-out customer's Snapshot purchase action. Live readback found the matching unverified pending claim bound to Google. Production enables Google, email, anonymous users and signup with the exact purchase callback allowed, but manual linking is disabled. The primary Google action used `linkIdentity`; the primary email action used guest `updateUser`. Switching a bound failed method hit `claim_busy` and became the reported generic verification error.

The purchase identity route now uses ordinary `signInWithOAuth` and `signInWithOtp(shouldCreateUser:true)` for new and existing accounts. It keeps the existing claim/capability graph to transfer access only after fresh verified platform authentication. No manual linking setting is enabled, no grant/RLS/ownership control is removed, and no payment or execution gate changes. Changing an unverified method or restarting an expired guest attempt uses existing cancel/reprepare RPCs with fresh capability and browser proofs, preserving the report and lineage. Verified/completed connections cannot be rewritten.

The original purchase-auth tab now recognises the verified target or a valid signed callback receipt and continues to the existing claim/resume flow. This makes the email-tab focus refresh useful for a distinct permanent account. Successful sign-in is announced while the signed report-bound checkout intent continues. An expired checkout intent returns to the same owned Snapshot purchase modal with a sign-in confirmation and requires renewed purchase choices. Authentication never charges a card, creates another Snapshot or bypasses checkout admission/idempotency.

Production email has a separate external blocker: both custom SMTP and Send Email hook were unconfigured at inspection. Supabase's built-in email sender is restricted to project-team recipients and two messages/hour; it does not establish public email delivery. See [official SMTP documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp). Existing Zoho OAuth contract-email credentials are not SMTP credentials. Keep confirmations enabled and do not purchase a service or fabricate successful email delivery. An existing entitled SMTP service must be configured securely before public email signup can be verified.

Regression runs the actual identity, callback, auth-tab, claim, resume and checkout-continuation routes with synthetic platform authentication. It covers new/existing Google and email accounts, failed-method rotation, expired claim, callback receipt retry, expired checkout intent, unavailable email, foreign identity, cross-origin and verified-binding rejection. The real SQL suite exercises cancelled method-switch proof rotation and denial of the old proof while retaining original report/history and RLS tests. Live Google/PKCE and actual inbox delivery require their own observed receipts; test mocks or a Ready deployment alone do not prove them. No new paid provider/AI/payment proof is needed.
