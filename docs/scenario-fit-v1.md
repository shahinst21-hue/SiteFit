# Scenario-fit evaluator v1 — owner-approved Phase 12 amendment

Approved by the owner on 11 October 2026. This is the shared decision contract for future Full Report editions. The Phase 11 Resident & Workplace Context Index remains a dated density-context index under D83; it is never presented as a commercial suitability or success score. No overall score is activated. Existing assessments and ready editions are immutable.

## Decision architecture

`scenario-fit-v1` consumes the admitted, version-bound Full Report fact catalog. A versioned operating-format registry enumerates coffee (takeaway-led, balanced, sit-in-led), restaurant (dine-in-led, takeaway-led, hybrid), hair (styling-led, multi-station, specialist), beauty (treatment-room, workstation, mixed) and explicit barber subtype (walk-in-led, appointment-led, hybrid) candidates. The original business type controls the registry; historical hair/beauty choices are not silently reclassified as barber.

Each candidate records required functional spaces, relevant trading occasions, physical state, conditional capacity or withholding reason, resident/workplace/access context, qualified inventory references, limitations and blocker evidence. A confirmed blocker excludes the affected format before comparison. No amount of favourable context overrides it. Missing or conflicting facts remain unknown. The evaluator does not infer customer numbers, storefront footfall, offer overlap, unique competitor totals, saturation, ratings, longevity, sales, permission or lease clearance from the available proxies.

| Concept | Formats compared | Relevant differentiation | Decision-critical physical check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee | Takeaway-led; balanced; sit-in-led | Morning/weekday context, residential repeat visits, actual offer overlap and complementary destinations | Counter/queue versus seating layout, preparation/washing and circulation |
| Restaurant | Dine-in-led; takeaway-led; hybrid | Lunch/evening occasions, actual cuisine/offer overlap and destination role | Kitchen/extraction, dining, collection, storage and waste |
| Barber | Walk-in-led; appointment-led; hybrid | Passing-trade hypothesis versus repeat appointments and observed service overlap | Stations, waiting, cleaning and circulation |
| Hair salon | Styling-led; multi-station; specialist | Appointment opportunity and specific service differentiation | Styling positions, washing, equipment and circulation |
| Beauty salon | Treatment-room; workstation; mixed | Service subtype and appointment opportunity | Privacy, hygiene, room/workstation geometry and relevant ventilation |

Commercial evidence is assessed by factor, with separate strength and effect. Dated resident and workplace measures support testing relevant occasions; they do not measure presence, conversion or spend. Named station activity and modelled routes support access hypotheses; they do not establish pedestrian direction past the frontage. Inventory requires entity matching, coverage, actual offer and hours before direct overlap can be asserted. Complementary clustering is a separate possibility. Reputation requires a lawful matched source, scale, date and sample; history requires an event with its own date meaning. First-seen records are not opening dates. These currently unsupported constructs stay `unverified` or `unavailable` in the scenario ledger. Premises/EPC/history and rent retain their own scope and limitations; rent is never converted to a Financial Engine conclusion.

The current admitted catalog lacks verified usable trading-unit layout and a source-specific current blocker rule. Therefore present real-source reports list candidates but withhold capacity and a leading format. Future source admission can pass reviewed exact-unit physical evidence to the evaluator; it must not treat EPC area, rental GIA, or an advertisement as usable layout. A total-area-only case can support labelled allocation scenarios after approval of their assumptions, never an exact maximum. A reviewed layout can support a conditional configuration count, bounded by independently verified applicable limits; it is not statutory occupancy approval.

Physical policy references: [RICS Code of Measuring Practice](https://www.rics.org/profession-standards/rics-standards-and-guidance/sector-standards/real-estate-standards/code-of-measuring-practice) distinguishes measurement bases; [Approved Document M](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/access-to-and-use-of-buildings-approved-document-m) informs access; [government fire-risk guidance](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-fire-safety-your-responsibilities/fire-risk-assessments) requires a premises-specific assessment; [FSA layout guidance](https://myhaccp.food.gov.uk/help/guidance/general-requirements-be-considered-each-prerequisite) informs food preparation flow; [HSE workplace space](https://www.hse.gov.uk/contact/faqs/roomspace.htm) and [beauty-service ventilation](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/guidance/sr13.pdf) inform service-specific checks. [Planning Portal use guidance](https://www.planningportal.co.uk/permission/common-projects/shops/i-want-to-convert-a-shop-to-a-cafe-public-house-or-takeaway/) illustrates why format and permitted use must be checked for the unit. None is a universal seats-per-square-metre or chairs-per-square-metre rule.

## Mathematical and missing-data policy

For each format, physical state is blocked, supported in a defined scope, or unresolved. Capacity requires a reviewed layout, compatible measured usable/NIA area, source IDs and a configured count. An independently evidenced limit may lower that count. Unknown limits remain qualifications; an unknown layout, incompatible area basis or total area alone withholds the number. Commercial context is a conditional hypothesis. Inventory records prompt an offer-overlap and complementary-clustering review but never count as automatic positive or negative points.

No numerical overall score is calculated. A later score would require a separately approved construct, component transforms, exclusions, fixed weights/interactions, correlation policy, calibration, missing-data publication gates and sensitivity validation. Missing components must not receive neutral values or redistributed weights. Evidence adequacy is distinct from attractiveness; a confirmed critical blocker prevents score publication. The Phase 11 index cannot be combined with its underlying resident/job operands as independent evidence.

Candidate future policies are (1) a fixed weighted utility with separately validated component transforms and interactions, and (2) a robust interval that reports the range over justified uncertainty and approved parameters. Neither has approved coefficients or a publication gate. A missing required component withholds the overall number; no denominator adjustment fills the gap. The chosen format and any future number must come from the same frozen evaluation and pass a consistency check.

Materiality order is confirmed blocker, decision-changing unresolved prerequisite, evidence changing the leading format, material counterevidence, then supporting context. The priority next action addresses the highest unresolved item that could change a lease decision. No positive/negative quota or one-finding-per-tab quota applies. Opposing evidence survives synthesis even if AI does not select it.

### Worked policy controls

These are synthetic cases, not SiteFit observations or professional area benchmarks.

| Case | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Small coffee layout with reviewed counter and four configured places; morning access/workplace context | Takeaway-led only if its physical configuration is uniquely supported; capacity is conditional and access remains a hypothesis |
| Large coffee layout with no relevant period evidence | Physical possibility, no commercial format winner |
| Restaurant with total GIA only and extraction unknown | No covers and no preferred cooking format |
| Restaurant with a confirmed applicable current operation restriction | Affected formats blocked even if resident/workplace context is favourable |
| Barber with reviewed stations but no waiting arrangement | Appointment-led may be the conditional lead only when its exact configuration and relevant context are admitted |
| Hair salon with obstructed four-station plan and reviewed three-station revision | Reject the four-position arrangement; three is conditional, not statutory occupancy |
| Beauty salon lacking treatment subtype or compatible unit area | Withhold capacity and format lead |
| Unauthorised terrace, conflicting area bases or competitor first-seen record | Exclude terrace capacity; preserve area conflict; do not infer competitor longevity |

## Output and Phase 13 handoff

The evaluator is frozen with each new preparation. Its decision and three-line Executive Summary are carried into the Overview and final report projection. The same record provides evidence-linked strengths, weaknesses, blocker visibility and one priority verification. Detailed tabs and future PDF exports read that immutable edition. The final synthesis AI receives the scenario decision but cannot change its leading format, capacity, score status or priority action. Existing Group A, Group B and synthesis calls remain the three normal calls. Source text in AI packets continues to pass the identity-removal guard.

The current three lines intentionally state unresolved format when source evidence does not distinguish candidates. The Overview must not present an AI-selected alternative as a supported recommendation. Phase 13's proposed Overview should render the consultant verdict, strengths, weaknesses, priority action, scenario comparisons, conditional capacity trace and source references from this shared projection. The later dashboard/PDF work remains separately gated.

## Verification boundaries

Pure-rule tests cover blocker precedence, no score, conditional capacity, total-area withholding, explicit barber subtype and rejection of non-premises capacity evidence. Full report regression and required live quality/lifecycle tests still govern Phase 12 completion. No historical assessment/report rewrite, provider purchase, Phase 13 implementation or Production activation is implied by this amendment.
