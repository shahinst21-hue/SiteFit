## Campaign 01 — coffee-shop lease decision

Audience hypothesis: London coffee-shop founders evaluating a specific lease. Proposed channel: tightly scoped intent-led search, subject to owner channel/budget approval. Destination: existing homepage, `?sf_campaign=campaign-01`. Scope landing copy to existing capabilities, not a new page.

Message A: “Before you sign for a coffee shop, understand the location.” Support: “Customer context, nearby business records, premises risks and the checks that could change your decision. London analysis. £29 Full Report.”

Message B: “What should you check before renting this coffee-shop premises?” Support: “Start with a Free Snapshot. Go deeper into the evidence and priority actions.”

Decision: do visitors with a real lease decision progress from useful Snapshot to confirmed purchase? Compare retained purchases and delivery outcomes, not clicks alone. Do not infer observed coffee demand from population. Exclude/avoid jobs, equipment-shopping and residential-property intent if the eventual chosen platform supports those controls.

## Common run card

Before Phase17 activation record: approved origin; creative; channel/source; dates; spending ceiling; pause rule; target audience; measurement consent/retention configuration; fulfilled-purchase and refund reconciliation; applicable launch-gate approval. No numeric success target or ad budget is invented.

Run sequentially initially. For each campaign retain platform/declared traffic and spend window, aggregate export/as-of timestamp, attribution coverage, confirmed/retained payments, Ready/delivery checks and requests. Small samples are directional, not statistical proof. A cheap click or an unpaid checkout is not willingness to pay £29. Pause if essential delivery/security/cost controls fail, regardless of conversion.

Owner decision after 2–3 campaigns: continue the strongest audience, fix an evidenced funnel obstacle, or stop and reconsider before further investment. Do not invest in new data/automation solely because traffic rose.
