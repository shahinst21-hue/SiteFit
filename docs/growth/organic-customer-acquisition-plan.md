# SiteFit organic customer acquisition plan

Prepared 13 November 2026. Owner decision pending. Every execution stage is **Not Started**. The £29 Full Report and Free Location Snapshot serve people evaluating a specific Greater London property. Start with café operators who have viewed or shortlisted a unit. Include restaurants through hospitality advisers when the same decision stage applies. The [readiness record](growth-readiness-improvements-20261113.md) documents release and measurement limits. The baseline was 19 paid clicks, one measured paid Snapshot view and no genuine purchase in the 3–13 November Click Test window.

## Outreach checks

[ICO business-to-business marketing guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/) distinguishes corporate subscribers from sole traders and some partnerships. Verify the actual recipient entity and subscriber class. A public form or inbox does not establish consent. Cold electronic marketing to an individual subscriber needs valid consent or an applicable soft opt-in. A new prospect is unlikely to qualify for soft opt-in. Check corporate status, route terms and objections before sending. Where classification is uncertain, skip the recipient or use a genuinely opt-in route. Social DMs are electronic mail under ICO guidance.

Identify SiteFit clearly, provide a working reply/opt-out and keep a minimal suppression record. Avoid named personal details where a role inbox suffices. If processing personal data, document the lawful basis, transparency and legitimate-interests assessment where relevant. Prefer a partner sharing the public link with clients through an appropriate relationship over requesting contact lists. Use no tracking pixels, no partner access to customer results, no proposed referral commission in the pilot, and no promise about the suitability of an agent's listing. [ICO planning guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/direct-marketing-guidance/plan-direct-marketing/)

### Scorecard

- **Qualified visit** means an operator with a named or shortlisted Greater London café/restaurant unit before lease commitment. Count aggregate confirmed introductions or consented visitors who reach address selection and choose an eligible business type as a proxy. Record confirmed and proxy counts separately. A click, partner reply or anonymous visit alone is not qualification.
- Record approved contacts, qualifying referrals, measured arrivals, checker starts, selected properties, Ready Snapshots, £29 offer views, checkout attempts, actual Checkout Sessions and genuine purchases. Count unique users where available. Use existing consented attribution and reconcile financial outcomes in Stripe and application records. Current campaign allowlists cannot distinguish these partners automatically. If safe fixed tagging is unavailable, keep unattributed outcomes separate and use aggregate partner-reported introductions without identifying data. Never add property identifiers to URLs or analytics.
- **Useful initial evidence** is three qualifying referrals, two completed Snapshots and one verified non-owner £29 purchase. Treat these as learning thresholds, not predictions. Count refunds or failed fulfilment separately and report net genuine purchases. One sale supports further testing, not a claim of repeatable acquisition.
- **Change direction** if nobody agrees to share after the first approved cohort and waiting window. Try tenant-side agents or marketplace editorial with approval. If qualified visitors reach the checker but fail to complete, investigate lookup, coverage and generation. If at least three qualified users view the offer and none attempt checkout, ask for consented feedback on value before changing price. If checkout starts without successful payments, check the relevant attempt and payment records before widening traffic.
- **Progress** at registration: no recipients approved, no messages sent, no introductions or pilot purchases recorded. Proposed pilot links, messaging version, dates, costs and outcome counts remain blank until an approved stage begins.
