## What stored London data can and cannot replace

- All14 admitted Production releases remain reusable under their stored provenance/licence: Census/geography/income/jobs, OS identifiers/coordinates, planning, place inventory and NUMBAT profiles. They are not a bulk archive of Postio addresses, per-property PropertyData facts or Geoapify routes. No Development customer source snapshots are copied into Production.
- A same owned report's exact stored context/source outcomes are replayed before new collection; Full preparation reads those snapshots and any already stored history/rental supplements. Ready reopening/dashboard/PDF do not refresh providers. Missing optional evidence is stored/disclosed/withheld, not converted into an assertion. Snapshot persistence/validation/ownership still have to pass.
- A new property's walking catchment needs qualified identity plus geometry before existing London operands can be spatially aggregated. Stored NUMBAT alone does not perform the current live StopPoint target discovery. Existing Postio-selected components/UDPRN are not a trusted UPRN mapping.
- PropertyData permits dated property-specific history and already-produced reports beyond60 days, but current data caching has a60-day limit; its terms also restrict continued serving after subscription ends. That is not blanket authority to use old Development records for a new commercial/current report. Geoapify permits storage of calculated isolines with applicable attribution/terms, but a London dataset does not contain those per-property results. TfL licences allow commercial reuse subject to attribution/conditions. Postio PAF capture and third-party reuse restrictions need focused confirmation for the actual SiteFit use; no general public address cache is authorised.
