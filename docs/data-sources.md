Recorded 2026-10-03. This is a candidate register, not a promise of integration, comprehensive UK coverage or permission for SiteFit's use. No adapter, API account or source dataset has been implemented or queried for a property.

Official overview pages were consulted for the references below on the record date. They establish only the stated broad capabilities. Every proposed use still requires verification of the exact dataset/endpoint, coverage across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, update frequency, cost, attribution, commercial use, retention and PDF/display rights before integration. Expected reliability is an assessment to validate, not a measured guarantee.

The NaPTAN and FSA overview fetches were unavailable during this review; their capabilities above remain explicitly unverified candidates. Geocoding is an approved architectural direction, not a verified integration. These limitations do not block Phase 0 documentation.

## Provider acceptance checklist for later phases

For each selected dataset/endpoint, record the official documentation and verified fields; sample coverage across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland; date and geography semantics; quota/cost; failure behaviour; attribution; commercial display, storage, reuse and export permissions; and implementation owner. Provider terms may constrain the proposed snapshot/evidence schema and PDF content. Resolve restrictions before promising report content.

All providers sit behind internal adapters when implemented. Keep provenance and availability states. Use the cheapest permitted source that supports the required claim rather than collecting unneeded data. No provider listed here is assumed to supply proprietary footfall, reliable turnover, guaranteed demand, complete premises history or a business success probability.

Premises history and demand can remain Unknown or Insufficient Evidence when permitted reliable observations are unavailable. The product does not build a national historical property database or scrape property websites to hide source gaps.

## UK-wide routing decision — 2026-10-04

This register is not a live-integration claim. Future adapters must declare nation/region/local-authority coverage; choose the appropriate permitted source for the resolved geography and report explicit gaps. TfL is a regional candidate only. ONS/Census must not be treated as a uniform four-nation feed without verification. Candidate additions for later investigation include National Records of Scotland, NISRA and nation-specific statistics/planning registers, plus national/regional transport sources (including Transport Scotland, Transport for Wales and Translink). Exact endpoints, coverage, terms and suitability are unverified and must be checked before integration. No accounts, source calls or provider adapters in Phase 2.5.

Source selection is driven by decision relevance, not large datasets. Distinguish registers from measured demand, station activity from premises footfall, and business records from survival/closure causes. Geographic variation and contradictory/stale evidence must survive normalisation into AI interpretation.
