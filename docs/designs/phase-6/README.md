## One Early View conclusion

**Repeat local custom looks plausible.**

Short reason: **Residential context supports a repeat-visit hypothesis.** Small, separate **Evidence: Limited** beside it. No demand/use instruction is mixed into the headline.

Below the conclusion, two distinct unresolved questions remain visible:

- **Trading-hour demand:** Who would visit, and when?
- **Permitted use:** Can this unit support your use? Checks have not run.

These are evidence gaps, not discovered constraints. The conclusion concerns a repeat-custom hypothesis, not sufficient current demand, whole-site viability or permission to sign a lease. Overall reasoning is available in its own closed disclosure with validated-section references required in production.

## Result, reason, then optional detail

Every factor: **result → short reason with small independent evidence strength → unresolved question → contextual Full Report question → closed Why this result?**

| Factor | Result | Short reason | Evidence | Result meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer base | **Repeat local custom looks plausible.** | Residential context supports a repeat-visit hypothesis. | Limited | Green: currently favours the scoped case. |
| Market position | **A distinctive offer could strengthen the case.** | More alternatives are mapped than in the matched set. | Limited | Yellow: trade-off/examination. |
| Customer access | **Public transport could support customer access.** | Tube and bus options are recorded nearby. | Limited | Blue: conditional/contextual. |
| Premises | **Premises evidence cannot yet support commitment.** | Use and lease checks have not run. | Insufficient | Grey: insufficient basis to conclude. |

Results replace instructions in the primary line. Conditional positioning/access are hypotheses supported by evidence plus reviewed business rules, not established competitive pressure, mandatory differentiation, measured uplift, practical journeys or a relative access advantage. Premises expresses the consequence of an unrun investigation, not an adverse local finding.

Colour describes the **result**, never evidence strength. The first three have identical Limited evidence and three different result tones. Evidence badges share a neutral treatment. Tone must follow the validated proposition, not a dimension name or strength enum. Text/icons retain the meaning without relying on colour. Lime buttons express the brand action, not an analytical result.

The large bottom reasoning panel is removed. All four factor disclosures start closed. Each contains supporting evidence, opposing evidence, alternative explanation, comparator, limitations, sources and expanded method/references. The market count/cohort comparison remains in its own disclosure. No opposing finding verified is not proof of no opposition; competitive concentration and destination clustering can coexist. The same result/reason/closed-Why pattern is required for future Full Report sections.

## Contextual continuation

| Factor | Unresolved question | Full Report continuation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer base | Who visits during your trading hours? | Which customers could fit your offer and hours? |
| Market position | Which offers compete, and which complement yours? | Which offers actually compete with yours? |
| Customer access | Can customers reach you at trading hours? | Does access work when you plan to trade? |
| Premises | Can this unit support your intended use? | What could stop or delay your intended use? |

Questions are investigation objectives, not guaranteed paid answers. Production requires actual unknowns, supporting Evidence IDs and approved capabilities. An unavailable capability cannot become a sales promise. Results and their bindings are frozen, not regenerated when opened.

## Strong locked financial benefit

**Can this location work financially?**

Preview three named outputs, without fake numbers or blurred generated findings:

- **Break-even sales** — sales needed to cover modelled costs.
- **Customers needed per day** — a daily target under the supplied average-spend assumptions.
- **Cost and rent sensitivity** — how changes affect the target.

**Run Financial Analysis** is a secondary outlined action; the final Full Case action remains dominant. The reference says **Opens the report outline; no payment or calculation.** There is no “Economics optional” marketing label, form, financial calculation or checkout.

Intended later path: unpaid → verified £29 Full Report purchase → main report → Economics inputs → explicit supplementary run. Already entitled users enter those inputs/run without another purchase. No second feature fee is introduced. Server-side entitlement, approved formulas, missing-input states and immutable input/model/output versions are required before activation. The main report never depends on financial inputs; use remains voluntary after purchase/main report. Opening a report/tab/preview performs no calculation or AI generation.

Financial outputs express scenario requirements, not achievable sales, measured unique customers, a forecast or a viability verdict. Daily people/transactions/visits depend on the approved average-spend unit and model. Preserve blank versus zero and make required assumptions clear in the eventual Economics area. This design does not move the financial engine into Phase 6.

## Primary CTA evaluation

| Candidate | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| **Check the Full Case · £29** — selected design candidate | Most explicit about investigating the fuller decision case, with the price visible. |
| Resolve the Key Unknowns · £29 | Strong motivation, but risks implying that every unknown will be resolved. |
| Check Before You Commit · £29 | Useful decision positioning, but less specific about the report being purchased. |

This is a conversion **hypothesis**, not a measured uplift or A/B result. Validate comprehension, completed purchases and report expectations when the paid path exists; clicks alone are not evidence of business value. £29, one-off payment and no subscription remain unchanged.

The final offer retains **See the case for — and against** and named decision benefits, including financial scenarios with the user's inputs. Customer fit and complementary trade remain untested investigations, not discovered opportunities. No invented threat, countdown, scarcity, discount, hidden finding or score uplift. The current primary action opens the outline with **Report outline only. Purchasing is not enabled.** Header navigation, Menu and Sample Report remain visual references.

## Layout and fixture

Desktop: Early View → four factors → full-width locked financial benefit → final Full Report offer. No sticky sidebar or detached reasoning panel. Mobile follows the same order, with one reserved-space fixed global action and safe-area CSS; short height returns it to flow. The full-scroll export restacks that action at the end to avoid obscuring content in a tall image. The separate viewport image shows actual fixed placement, not another product flow.

24 Example Street, London is fictional. All analysis is illustrative: synthetic 480 residents / 4.0 hectares / 12,000 residents per km², upper-quartile OA context, 8 mapped alternatives / median 5 / 64 anchors, and bus/Tube register observations. Demo E01–E04 are aliases, not application UUIDs or live proofs. Production needs same-context, supporting Evidence IDs; existence of an ID alone never proves the claim.

Stronger copy must not outrun evidence. Current demand, POI/comparator usefulness, postcode precision, Census age, live semantic AI evaluation, guest policy, model/provider terms/cost, financial formulas and paid delivery remain unproved gates. No missingness or Definition of Done requirement is weakened. No new provider, service, account or production hardening is introduced.
