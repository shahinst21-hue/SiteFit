# Phase16 — SEO, Analytics and Marketing Infrastructure

Owner-approved implementation, 13 October 2026. Development/protected Preview only. No advertising, paid providers/AI, Production/indexing activation, Live payments, main merge or Phase17 implementation. Phase15 remains Owner-Accepted / Closed with Exceptions.

## Scope and execution

16.0 baseline: reuse delivered Phase12 Ready/replay, Phase13 dashboard, Phase14 fulfilment and Phase15 controls; no repeated editorial/security redesign.

16.1 public/private indexing: existing metadata, sitemap and Blog read repository; add explicit report/purchase/internal/dev robots exclusions and private dev headers. Preview stays noindex and authenticated. Check query-free canonicals, published-only sitemap, anonymous private responses. Robots is not access control.

16.2 claims: qualify London-only analysis versus UK address acceptance; remove embedded optional-economic and unsupported competitor-offer promises; preserve separate Financial Engine and indicative analysis.

16.3 measurement: three allowlisted campaign codes, optional independent affirmative consent and HTTP-only session cookie. No default acceptance, pixel, fingerprint, session replay, raw query/referrer, property details or visitor identifier. Cookie removal stops subsequent collection; browser restoration may retain a session cookie. Private analysis association derives from verified session/owned Ready free report, first-touch immutable per analysis, purchase start recorded once. Fresh 15-minute admission window is an attribution safety bound, not a data-retention period. Old reports/analyses are not retroactively attributed. Measurement failures have a bounded 750 ms optional observer and cannot fail the product. Existing trusted payments/reports supply conversion outcomes; no webhook changes or paid dispatch.

16.4 results: service-only aggregate RPC and local JSON-to-Markdown CLI. No analytics dashboard. Cohort is earliest Ready free-report creation in [from,to); counts are analyses/payments, not unique people. Outcomes are current as-of export time and can change. Channel traffic and spend are explicit owner inputs; missing values are Unknown. Requests are not refunds; Ready is not proof of receipt. Partial/pending reversals/disputes withhold net receipts. Fees/tax/report costs excluded. No unique-customer CAC or profit claim.

16.5 campaign kit: two concept-specific search hypotheses and one winner-follow-up, existing routes/brand, no new pages. Final channel, spend cap and stopping criteria require owner approval before activation.

16.6 verification/delivery: locked install/check, new schema PostgreSQL/privacy/idempotence tests, real HTTP consent/private-indexing smoke, public routes, public mobile/tablet/desktop/keyboard check, exact-head CI and protected Preview; development PR only. Fixtures and hosted observations are separately recorded.

## Completion contract

Deliver verified Development/Preview implementation, event/attribution definitions, reproducible results example, campaign briefs and Phase17 handover. Test-only metrics are not real willingness-to-pay evidence. Optional production measurement remains hard-disabled; final approved retention/privacy and Live source integration belong to separately authorised Phase17. No old reservations released, report/customer history changed or source recollected.

## Explicit limits

No first-party visit/checker-start collector; those totals come from declared channel records and must not be passed off as measured funnel users. No historical backfill, cross-device attribution or guaranteed session persistence across browser restarts. Source/medium are campaign configuration, not independently verified acquisition facts. Campaign codes can be manually copied and attribution is not fraud-proof. Measurement consent is separate from immediate-supply consent. Private association records need an approved retention/data-rights criterion before commercial collection; no duration invented. Current payment schema is Test-only; Production conversion read integration requires Phase17 verification. Unresolved launch dependencies remain in launch-gate-register.md and do not reopen Phase15.
