# SiteFit customer copy — 14 October 2026

Write for the independent owner who has found a property and is deciding whether to commit. The central question is **“What should I know before signing?”** Current supported concepts are coffee shop, restaurant, hair salon and beauty salon; Hair/Beauty share the approved analytical category. Do not market unsupported independent-retail analysis.

## Voice

Natural British English: clear, calm, specific and commercially aware. Explain the implication before the detail. Prefer short sentences, familiar nouns and a concrete next action. Premium means useful and understandable, not inflated language. Avoid generic “powerful insights”, “revolutionise”, “perfect location”, “smart decisions” and repeated “This suggests”. Do not claim a conversion improvement without a measured outcome.

## Terminology

| Use | Meaning / guardrail |
|---|---|
| Property / premises | The selected unit; use “premises” for layout, use and works |
| Location | The property and surrounding context, not a guaranteed trading opportunity |
| Business type | Coffee shop, restaurant, hair salon or beauty salon |
| Free Snapshot | Free initial assessment; no account, card or financial-input prerequisite |
| Full Report | One configured £29 report; eight sections, saved account access and PDF when Ready |
| Customer context | Residents, jobs and demographic context; not observed buying demand |
| Modelled walking area | Possible routes/area from the stored model; not measured visits |
| Nearby business records | Register leads, not automatically current direct competitors |
| Rent & Lease | Qualified rent estimates/benchmarks and lease checks; no Financial Engine calculations |
| Unknown | Evidence cannot answer the question; never equivalent to zero or “no risk” |
| Estimated / modelled | Keep the actual source basis, date, units and limitations |
| Appendix | Source references, methods, lineage and detailed limitations |

Keep statutory contractual wording intact. Never replace source qualifications with broad “verified”, “accurate”, “complete”, “live” or “up to date” claims. No fictional testimonials, customer counts, affiliations, ratings, capacity, rent, success scores or measured shopfront footfall.

## Journey

- Home: the property decision, London coverage, one free next step, a secondary sample.
- Forms: what to enter, why it is needed, what to do after an error. Do not promise completion within an unverified time.
- Snapshot: provide its actual initial findings, then explain the additional Full Report value. No artificially withheld free explanation.
- Purchase: configured price, one-off charge, actual account and consent requirements, actual payment/supply states. Payment confirmed is not report delivered.
- Recovery: explain the saved state and the next action; never encourage a second purchase to solve failed fulfilment.
- Report/PDF: preserve the three-line Verdict, ranked strengths/risks, one priority action, four ranked actions and eight sections. Material uncertainty stays beside its finding.
- Examples: visibly fictional or development review. Do not strengthen illustrative or frozen sourced drafts into Ready reports.
