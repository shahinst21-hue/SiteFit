# Consultant report model v1

Accepted owner direction, 11 October 2026. Implementation candidate in the Phase 12 branch; live revised-content quality and owner acceptance remain open. This supersedes atom-only customer prose for **new** Full preparations, not immutable ready reports or numerical methods.

## Commercial answer and content

The report must explain the selected property's opportunity, material opposition and decision-changing next step for the selected concept. Its Executive Summary has a three-line Consultant Verdict, prioritised Key Strengths, Key Weaknesses/Risks and one Priority Action. Four ranked actions retain a specific consequence: what would change the lease or operating decision if the answer were adverse. Every page supplies a conclusion, substantive analysis and a falsifiable decision test. Generic caution repetition is not content acceptance.

The existing two parallel grouped calls select evidence focus. A third, dependent GPT 6.1 Sol synthesis writes all eight pages and the Executive Summary from the **entire admitted frozen catalog**, not just the selected headline atoms. No extra model judge or automatic rewrite loop. Source strings are untrusted. Numerical facts, weights, transforms, scenario gates, readiness and financial arithmetic remain outside AI.

`consultant-narrative-v1` is strict structured interpretation with fact/operator/supplement references. Numeric statements use bound fact or rental slots; names use bound operator slots. Runtime rejects foreign references, literal figures, private identifiers, unsupported format recommendations and malformed output. Rendering supplies rounded values. **Reference validation is not semantic entailment or evidence of £29 quality**: the three-concept real content review and owner acceptance remain mandatory. Hypotheses must state what would confirm or reject them. An interpretation cannot establish a newly observed fact.

## Competition: individual operators and truthful gaps

Coffee-shop and restaurant pages must separately address:

- Complete unique competitor total, or why it is unavailable. A returned register record, shortlist entry or native map record is not a unique active direct competitor.
- Exact operator/branch, registered category, location precision and dated provenance.
- Source-verified offer and occasion overlap, price position and trading periods where admitted.
- Verified brand/chain affiliation, rather than an association inferred from a name.
- Customer reputation with rating scale, review count, source and date. **FSA hygiene is a separate dated inspection result**, never a customer review or competitive-strength score.
- The resulting commercial comparison and what remains unassessed. Missing reviews do not mean weak competition; a recognised name alone does not establish a strong competing proposition.

`register-shortlist-v2` excludes a clearly matching selected-premises name, preserves six frozen provider-order leads and adds six cafe/restaurant/pub category leads. It deduplicates only native register identity, not nearby points or similar names. It is bounded, not nearest-first, complete or ranked by commercial strength. Original source snapshots remain unchanged. New FSA acquisition may retain validated dated FHRS hygiene under existing licence; old snapshots are not retrofitted or refetched.

Additional attributes must first pass ordinary source admission with exact trading-unit identity, source verification, dates, licence/retention and evidence lineage. The new comparison contract consumes those fields; it does **not** currently acquire verified offers/reviews automatically. FSA cannot establish salon competition.

The existing web-search adapter can formulate a bounded competition discovery request. `competitionDiscoveryNeeds` records gaps and the paid-preparation-only dispatch/admission gate. Search returns **candidates**, not automatically admitted facts. No web dispatch was made for this amendment and no new search spending is authorised. Exact-branch source matching and reuse permissions remain necessary; public availability is not a blanket retention licence. Route's currently found menu names other branches; a London Levain name cannot establish affiliation with a foreign chain. These pilot observations are not stored report facts.

## Concept-specific operating comparison

Restaurants distinguish hot-food kitchen/extraction, weekday lunch, evening dining, usable seating/customer facilities, collection/delivery and operating restrictions. Compare their trade-offs rather than relabel coffee copy. Coffee distinguishes repeat local visits, morning/weekday convenience and counter-versus-seating needs. Salons distinguish intended services, repeat appointments and equipment/service layout.

The approved `scenario-fit-v1` gates remain binding. Without compatible, verified exact-unit permission/layout, no leading operating format, seating/covers, workstations or usable capacity is invented. A larger context index cannot override a premises blocker. Trading occasions are hypotheses, not measured customers, delivery demand or a profit forecast.

## Evidence and customer presentation

Use all **relevant admitted** resident, demographic, employment, income, access, premises, history and rental evidence. Do not pad content with unsupported source outcomes or weaker substitutes. TS007A all usual residents and TS066 residents aged sixteen-plus retain separate universes. Cumulative walking contours are not summed. Native employment is not walking customers or daytime population; native modelled household income is not catchment spending. Selected property's asking/contracted rent, market benchmark and compatible estimate remain distinct; the frozen first case has no admitted rental conclusion.

Customer prose uses approximate counts, rounded money and route minutes; exact operands, periods, comparators, missing states and provenance remain frozen. Millions use a readable approximate scale, without falsely saying “over” when rounding upward. Ratings retain appropriate precision. Contextual material qualifications accompany their conclusions; detailed methodology lives in Appendix. Required cautions are retained, not discarded to make copy optimistic.

The final narrative replaces redundant legacy selected prose in new section exports. Accepted group selections remain in the checkpoint/preparation. Deterministic cautions, evidence bindings and missingness remain available. Lossless `stored-profiles-v3` sharing preserves cell values/contributions/shares/reasons and historical codecs; malformed references fail closed. No limit is increased to admit fabricated or truncated evidence.

## Execution and historical integrity

New preparation binds `consultant-narrative-v1`; old preparation and ready content retain their original replay contract. Reopening performs no source retrieval, model dispatch, rescoring or report rewriting. A materially new run needs a new immutable edition and explicit spending authority. Existing before-dispatch durable intent, CAS checkpoints and ambiguous-outcome protection remain authoritative.

The three real narrative review attempts all reached the old 60-second timeout without response/usage. This concrete failure changes the synthesis timeout to 120 seconds and route configuration to 200 seconds, leaving room for parallel 45-second groups and publication. Actual deployed 200-second behavior remains unverified. A stale intent is still protected for 180 seconds **from that dispatch's reservation**, longer than its bounded provider call. No automatic retry or paid semantic repair is enabled for this new edition. Historical atom editions retain their existing shared repair behavior.

Bounds: group request 20 KB / 3,000 output tokens; synthesis request 28 KB / 6,000 output tokens. Nominal three-call byte-based planning ceiling is US$0.256 plus conservative wrapper allowance, within the US$0.30 report reservation at the recorded [GPT 6.1 Sol tariff](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6.1-sol). This is a conservative estimate, not actual billing or a promise about provider latency. Aggregate phase budget still US$1; failed or ambiguous requests retain their reservation. The owner selected editorial review only; proposed supplementary US$0.60 was **not approved**. No further paid request follows.
